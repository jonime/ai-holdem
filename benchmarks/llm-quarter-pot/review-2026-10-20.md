## Interpretation and artifacts

This is retrospective screening of two selected situations, not independent
confirmation or measured playing strength. Values are exact conditional on the
frozen synthetic ranges and responses; there are no sampling confidence intervals.
Ten repetitions do not establish stable LLM action frequencies. Posterior-best is
an optimistic range-informed reference, not an observed LLM response. The failure
does not prove quarter-pot universally inferior and authorizes no spending or
production promotion. No profiles, ranges, continuations or observations were
retuned or added after viewing results.
