## Mechanical findings and premium-hand diagnosis

All 180 controlled Rules fixture/difficulty/representation checks pass for the
candidate. Exactly four actions differ from baseline: the previously identified
medium/hard turn-draw seat/amount variants. The 3,840-observation sweep covers all
24 global suit permutations, sixteen hand numbers, five representations and both
medium/hard. For each hand number/difficulty, candidate actions agree across
representations and suits. Sixteen distinct frequencies remain across the sixteen
hand numbers. The observed sweep proportions are not target mixing frequencies
and its transformed rows are not independent poker situations.

The medium six-seat UTG call threshold is 45%: 40% immediate contestable-pot odds
plus the existing 5% margin. Equity is random-hand showdown share against all five
active opponents. The active-opponent count agrees with the situation projection
in all 165 traced cases; no count/arithmetic correction was demonstrated.

These observations explain the branch choices, not their strategic value. The
policy compares multiway random showdown share with an immediate price; it does
not model future folds, contributions, realization or alternative action EV.
Lowering a threshold solely to force a preferred action would not validate a fix.
No preflop threshold candidate was introduced. Value raising additionally faces
existing 60%/profile gates, which these five-way equity estimates do not reach.

## Recommendations and verification

- **Rules seed — defer production promotion.** Keep the canonical seed and its mechanical regression coverage. It removes four known representation differences and preserves hand-number variation. Match intervals do not establish a strength improvement; the rewrite also reassigns deterministic choices on ordinary hands.
- **Rules preflop — defer a strategy patch.** No active-opponent or price defect was demonstrated. A future isolated preflop strategy experiment needs independent action-value assumptions and evidence; do not promote the earlier losing bundled challenger or force folds to disappear by changing expectations.
- **Jev normalization — defer promotion.** Semantic inputs become consistent, but the premium-pair sizing concern remains. This pilot provides no action-value evidence supporting a production change.
- **LLM normalization — defer promotion.** Identical canonical inputs still produce variable actions/sizes. Reduced input variation is a useful benchmark contract, but does not establish stronger or suitably mixed play. No further calls follow automatically.
