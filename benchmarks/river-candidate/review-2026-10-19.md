# River candidate v1 — saved-evidence review, 2026-10-19

**Final verdict: defer easy, medium and hard. No production promotion.**

The manual development stage completed all 48 independent situations, shared across the three difficulties (144 situation/difficulty blocks). Every difficulty failed the frozen requirement for a positive development mean in all nine range/opponent cells: polarized versus current Rules was negative. Eight of nine cells had positive means for each difficulty. No difficulty qualified for confirmation, so the confirmation command correctly produced an empty selected set and zero confirmation simulations. There is no 120-situation confirmation result and no confirmation strength claim.

All difficulties had zero failed blocks, passing frozen mechanical checks (24 checks at the 5,000-sample budget), and activation in every stratum. Candidate activation was 33/48 (68.75%) for each difficulty. The 15 inactive situations include 12 preallocated forced splits and three additional unchanged baseline bets. Those three saved roots were 14/42, 18/54 and 8/24 chips (one-third pot), outside the half-pot treatment predicate. All inactive observations remain in the means with zero paired treatment effect.

No alternative candidate, extra samples, narrowed predicate or source edits were used to rescue the result. Production Rules, the existing KK improvement, Jev, Eevo, LLMs, context and persistence remain unchanged.
