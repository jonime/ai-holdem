<h2 id="help-section-1">Hand rankings</h2>

Use the best five cards from your two hole cards and the five community cards. From strongest to weakest:

1. **Straight flush: five consecutive cards of one suit** — <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="9♠ 8♠ 7♠ 6♠ 5♠" />.
2. **Four of a kind: four cards of the same rank** — <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="A♠ A♥ A♦ A♣ K♠" />.
3. **Full house: three of a kind and a pair** — <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="K♠ K♥ K♦ 2♣ 2♥" />.
4. **Flush: five cards of one suit** — <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="A♥ J♥ 8♥ 5♥ 2♥" />.
5. **Straight: five consecutive ranks** — <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="9♠ 8♥ 7♦ 6♣ 5♠" />.
6. **Three of a kind: three cards of the same rank** — <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="Q♠ Q♥ Q♦ 9♣ 2♥" />.
7. **Two pair: two different pairs** — <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="J♠ J♥ 4♦ 4♣ A♥" />.
8. **One pair: two cards of the same rank** — <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="10♠ 10♥ A♦ 7♣ 3♥" />.
9. **High card: no combination above** — <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="A♠ J♥ 8♦ 5♣ 2♥" />.

A royal flush is the highest straight flush: <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="A♠ K♠ Q♠ J♠ 10♠" /> (ace, king, queen, jack and ten of spades). A can also be low in A–2–3–4–5. Higher ranks and then the remaining cards (kickers) decide ties; identical best five-card hands split the pot. Suits do not break ties. Symbols: <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="♠" /> spades, <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="♥" /> hearts, <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="♦" /> diamonds, <InlineCardExample labels={dictionary.cards} cards="♣" /> clubs. A = ace, K = king, Q = queen, J = jack.

<h2 id="help-section-2">Betting basics</h2>

- Fold: give up your hand and any claim to the pot; chips already paid stay in the pot.
- Check: pay nothing and pass the action when no bet needs calling.
- Call: match the current bet, paying only the chips still owed, up to your remaining stack.
- Bet: open the betting when there is no current bet.
- Raise: increase the current bet. The amount field is your total contribution on this betting street, not extra chips to pay. Example: you have paid 100, the current bet is 200, and you raise to 600. You pay 500 more (600 − 100), not 600. Calling would cost 100.
- All-in: commit your remaining stack. You can win only the pots you contributed to. Extra chips from other players form side pots for eligible contributors; going all-in does not mean being eliminated before the hand is resolved. The controls show the legal actions and sizing limits. The All-in sizing button selects the maximum; submit it with Bet/Raise or D.

<h2 id="help-section-3">Keyboard shortcuts</h2>

- A: fold. S: check or call. D: submit the selected bet or raise amount.
- Q / ←: decrease the target by one big blind. E / →: increase it by one big blind. Hold Shift for five big blinds. Targets stay within legal limits.
- After a hand ends, S / Enter / Space starts the next hand only when eligible; D shows your cards only when showing is available after a hand won by folds. These keys do not automatically start another hand.
- Actions work only on your eligible owned human turn; next-hand and show shortcuts follow their button eligibility. They are unavailable during pending requests, recovery, or an expired turn. Spectators cannot act for players.
- Shortcuts are ignored in text or number inputs, text areas, selects and editable content (the range slider allows them), while a dialog is open, with Ctrl/Alt/Meta, or for repeated key presses. Enter/Space keeps its normal behavior on focused buttons and links.


Turn sound and gameplay shortcuts can be changed in Settings. Turning off gameplay shortcuts hides their hints and disables action keys and custom bet adjustments. Tab navigation, Enter/Space on focused buttons, number editing, and native slider keys still work. Language changes keep you at the same table and are available once requests and recovery finish.

In Settings, Card colours defaults to Classic. Four-colour keeps spades dark and hearts red, with blue diamonds and green clubs. Suit symbols stay the same.

**Show current hand** is on by default in Settings. Beneath your hole cards, it shows your current made-hand category from the flop onward, using only your cards and the revealed board. This describes the hand you have now, not its chance of winning. All-ins still show it; folded or departing players do not. Your active seat says **Your turn**. At completion, the usual showdown results replace the live label. Changes apply immediately and are saved in this browser.


In Settings, choose Amount display: Chips or Big blinds. This changes presentation only. Enter bets in integer chips; presets, shortcuts and submitted actions still use exactly the same chips. The target total and additional amount paid remain distinct. Big-blind amounts show at most two decimals, omit trailing zeros, and use ≈ for rounded values. Nonzero amounts smaller than 0.01 BB show &lt;0.01 BB, retaining the gain/loss direction. Actions and replay use each hand’s original big blind. Older history and frozen shared replays without that metadata show labelled chips; replay explains when the blind size is unavailable.

<h2 id="help-section-4">Review hand</h2>

Choose **Review hand** from a completed hand in Actions. Review starts after blinds. Use Previous/Next or the left/right arrow keys within review, street buttons, and Result to inspect actions, stacks, bets, the board and pot awards. Street buttons select the first frame of that street, including automatic all-in runouts. The player who performed the selected action is highlighted. On mobile, Back to Actions restores your reading position.

Replays open paused at the first frame. **Play / Pause** sits between Previous and Next. Playback waits 1.2 seconds per frame, or 2 seconds on the first frame and frames revealing community cards. It stops at Result; **Replay** starts again from the beginning. Manual navigation pauses playback. Hiding the tab or opening another dialog also pauses it; press Play explicitly to resume with a fresh delay. Amount and card-colour changes preserve the frame, playback and timing. These controls also work in shared replays.

Your own cards remain visible throughout. Other cards appear only at Result when the completed-hand rules allow them (showdown or an authorized reveal); folded or mucked cards stay hidden otherwise. Review uses the same 50-hand Actions window. Older incomplete records may be unavailable. Review never pauses bots, updates or turn timers: a timed turn can expire and fold. Your turn is highlighted beside Close; close the review to return to the live table. Closing shows the latest live table. No actions can be submitted through review.

<h2 id="help-section-5">Shared replay</h2>

Choose **Share hand** beside **Close** in the review header. Closing the sharing dialog returns to the same replay frame.

Your cards (including folded cards) and all bots’ cards will be visible from the start. Other humans’ cards appear only if public at showdown or explicitly shown. Anyone with the link can forward it. This fixed snapshot expires in 30 days; later Show actions do not update it.
